## 26 A5 Pattern Analysis in Encoded Script Blocks

### Overview
The `26 A5` byte pattern appears in only 2 of 38 asset blocks in the cartridge, and appears to be a compressed palette reference format related to the palette address $02:A526.

### Occurrences

#### Root block (bank $01): 4 occurrences
- **offset 0E8D** (prog rel 0E6D from entry):
  - 4 bytes after `26 A5`: `6A EF D7 A2`
  - 4 bytes before `26 A5`: `D7 73 2E 40` (includes `40` opcode)
  
- **offset 0EDC** (prog rel 0EBC from entry):
  - 4 bytes after `26 A5`: `6A EF D7 F1`
  - 4 bytes before `26 A5`: `8E BB B0 DD`
  - `26 A5` appears directly (without preceding `40`)

- **offset 610C** (prog rel 60EC from entry):
  - 4 bytes after `26 A5`: `93 F1 D7 3B`
  - 4 bytes before `26 A5`: `D7 15 81 40` (includes `40` opcode)
  - Note: `40 26 A5` pattern present

- **offset 616F** (prog rel 614F from entry):
  - 4 bytes after `26 A5`: `93 F1 D7 C2`
  - 4 bytes before `26 A5`: `D7 78 81 40` (includes `40` opcode)
  - Note: `40 26 A5` pattern present

#### Koueki block (bank $08): 1 occurrence
- **offset 0640** (prog rel 0590 from entry):
  - 4 bytes after `26 A5`: `69 EF 5E B5`
  - 4 bytes before `26 A5`: `27 0A 8F 12`
  - Followed by: `5E B5 8C B4 E6 BB 29 09 B0 25 40 2B 38 E9 4E 88 02 D8 8C B6`
  - Note: `02` appears near the end and `40 2B` appears after `B0 25`

### Key Patterns

1. **`40 26 A5`** appears in 3 of 4 root occurrences before `26 A5`. The `40` opcode (script VM "op $40") may indicate "load palette using 26 A5 address".

2. **After `26 A5`**, the next bytes are consistently `6A EF` or `69 EF` - these appear to be 16-bit operands or offsets.

3. The `A5` byte is the high byte of the palette address $A526. The `26` is the low byte.

4. In koueki block, after `26 A5 69 EF`, the sequence continues with `5E B5 8C B4 E6 BB 29 09 B0 25 40 2B 38 E9 4E 88 02 D8 8C B6` - here `02` appears near the end and `40 2B` appears after `B0 25`.

### Interpretation
`26 A5` appears to be a 2-byte compressed palette reference in the encoded script format. The full palette address $02:A526 is referenced with `26 A5`, where the bank byte $02 is either:
- Implied from context (palette table at $02:A526)
- Stored separately in the script VM state
- Part of a 3-byte encoding where the third byte is sometimes omitted

The `40` opcode preceding `26 A5` in most cases suggests this is a palette load/reference instruction in the script VM's encoded format.
